### 3b. Held-out test: the honest picture

The voice model is a small logistic regression on voice features, trained on 739 clips (218 human, 521 synthetic). Every clip, human and synthetic, was also given random room reverb, noise, band-limiting and sample-rate changes, so the model cannot win just by spotting a different microphone. These 216 clips were never used for training.

| Held-out slice | Clips | AUC (0.5 = coin flip, 1 = perfect) | Synthetic caught | Real voices wrongly flagged |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All held-out clips | 216 | 0.674 | 32/107 (30%) | 16/109 (15%) |
| Clean direct recordings | 57 | 0.81 | 15/29 (52%) | 4/28 (14%) |
| Simulated phone line | 32 | 0.641 | 8/16 (50%) | 6/16 (38%) |
| Noisy / reverberant / band-limited | 127 | 0.609 | 9/62 (15%) | 6/65 (9%) |

Per group, judged at the product's "be careful" threshold:

| Group | Truth | Clips | Judged correctly |
|---|---|---|---|
| human sentences | human | 20 | 10/20 (50%) |
| human words | human | 89 | 71/89 (80%) |
| Kokoro sentences | synthetic | 36 | 15/36 (42%) |
| espeak words | synthetic | 24 | 18/24 (75%) |
| Kokoro words | synthetic | 47 | 18/47 (38%) |

**What this means.** Voice detection from simple signal features is only moderately better than chance on unseen audio (and clearly better on clean direct recordings). The product therefore labels this check *experimental*, never tells a user a voice is "genuine", and sets its thresholds to avoid false accusations at the cost of missing many clones. A trained deep anti-spoofing model (for example AASIST or a fine-tuned wav2vec 2.0) is the next step; it could not be downloaded in the build environment.

## Limits you should know about

- Documents: the checks catch edited numbers, broken arithmetic, editing-software traces and invalid ID numbers. The specimen files were written to contain these specific traces, so 11/11 shows the checks work as designed, not how often real forgers leave such traces. A forger who produces a perfectly consistent document with a clean generator will pass. A pass means "no tampering signs found", never "genuine".
- Liveness: the table above uses prepared measurements, so it shows the decision logic, not camera accuracy. Camera accuracy needs real recordings of real people and real attacks.
- Voice: the training data is small and public (human speech from Google Speech Commands plus ten sentence recordings; synthetic speech from Kokoro and espeak). Commercial cloning tools (ElevenLabs, RVC and others) were not tested. The sample rows in 3a include clips the model saw in training, so use the held-out table in 3b for the real accuracy.
- All test documents and numbers are made up for testing.
